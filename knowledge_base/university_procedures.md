# Academic & Administrative Procedures Guide

## 1. Attendance Shortage Application Procedure
- **Minimum Requirement:** 75% attendance is required to sit for semester final examinations without penalty.
- **Between 60% and 74% (Non-Collegiate):** 
  - Must submit a formal application to the Head of Department (HOD) or Academic Coordinator.
  - Attach medical certificates or valid proof of emergency/leave.
  - Pay the prescribed non-collegiate fine at the accounts office before collecting the admit card.
- **Below 60% (Discollegiate):**
  - Not eligible to sit for regular semester final exams without special academic council dispensation.

## 2. Make-Up Exam & Class Test (CT) Rules
- If a student misses a Class Test (CT) due to genuine illness or family emergency:
  - Must inform the Class Representative (CR) within 24 hours of the missed exam.
  - Submit application along with valid documentation directly to the course teacher.
  - Course teachers hold make-up CTs during designated review weeks or consultation hours.

## 3. University Working Days & Weekly Off Days
- **Regular Class Days:** Designated weekdays as per the university academic routine.
- **Weekly Off Days:** Standard university weekends and declared public holidays.
- **University Office Hours:** 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM on official working days.

## 4. Admit Card Clearance Procedure
1. Clear all semester tuition dues at the accounts office.
2. Obtain online clearance on the student portal.
3. Download and print the admit card prior to the examination week.
